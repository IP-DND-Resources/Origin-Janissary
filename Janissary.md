# Origin: Janissary
The Janissary was conscripted at a young age, meant to serve and protect a master that was chosen for them. They spend their youth training to fight and being taught how to serve without question. Some find comfort in the structure and given purpose, others escape their conscription to pursue their goals. Regardless of the path they take, the combat prowess of the Janissary is a force to be reckoned with. Their skills with weapons or magic have been instilled in them through rigorous training their entire life. Where many choose to pick up the sword, the Janissary views the sword as an extension of themselves.

\column

**Ability Scores:** Dexterity, Constitution, Intelligence

**Feat:** Tough (See PHB chapter 5)

**Skill Proficiencies:** Acrobatics and Athletics

**Tool Proficiency:** Weavers Tools (See PHB chapter 6)

**Equipment:** *Choose* A or B: (A) Chain, 2 Handaxe, Iron Spikes, Light Hammer, Manacles, Pouch, Rope, Shovel, 2 Spear, Tent, Traveler's Clothes, Weaver's Tools, 20 GP; or (B) 50 GP
